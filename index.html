Scan Line Camera v1

Center-line sampling experiment for iPhone
Camera off
30 lines/s
The preview shows the normal camera view only for visualization. The white vertical line is the active one-dimensional scan line. Every received sensor line is appended directly beside the previous line. There is no physical-position, movement-speed, interpolation, or resampling logic in the reconstruction. Scan rate is the simulated fixed line-acquisition rate. The eventual hardware can provide one TCD1304-style line at a time, independently of its electronic integration time. Camera access requires HTTPS.
