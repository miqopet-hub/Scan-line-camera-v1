Center-line sampling experiment for iPhone

The preview shows the normal camera view only for visualization. The white vertical line is the active one-dimensional scan line. Every received sensor line is appended directly beside the previous line. There is no physical-position, movement-speed, interpolation, or resampling logic in the reconstruction. The slider sets the requested line-acquisition rate. In this iPhone browser prototype, actual acquisition is limited by browser scheduling and the camera video frame rate; selecting 1000 does not guarantee 1000 distinct camera lines per second. Image data is stored in fixed-width chunks, so the app does not resize and recopy the entire image for every new line. Very long captures remain limited by available device memory. Wide captures export as multiple numbered PNG sections. Camera access requires HTTPS.
