The app samples only the vertical line at the centre of the iPhone camera view. Each sample becomes one vertical strip of the photograph. Scan rate controls how often a line is captured; movement speed controls how far the virtual camera travels between samples. This is a simulation of the future linear-CCD camera, not a true line sensor. Camera access requires HTTPS.
